Source-page anomaly: the TAU index lists three Habakkuk text pages. The captured page 2 is titled/indexed as Habakkuk 2, but its twenty BibleGateway links target Nahum 2:1–20. The archive preserves the exact page and per-label target without correcting or resolving the mismatch.
