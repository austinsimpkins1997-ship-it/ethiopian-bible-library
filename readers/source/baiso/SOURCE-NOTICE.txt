Baiso New Testament editions — source and rights notice

Language: Baiso (Bayso), IANA tag bsw. Both eBible.org edition pages identify The Word for the World International as contributor and state CC BY-SA 4.0 terms for translation text. The provider's Latin-script package (BSWLTW / bswl) contains 16 USFM books; the Ethiopic-script package (BSWETW / bswe) contains 27. These are separate editions and their texts are not merged.
