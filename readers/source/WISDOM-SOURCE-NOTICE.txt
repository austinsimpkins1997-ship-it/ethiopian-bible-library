No TEI source extract or reader text is included in this public repository. A captured
copy is retained outside the public repository for local audit. The source file states
CC BY-SA 4.0 at file level, while the current Beta maṣāḥǝft guidance states
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and a dataset record carries CC BY 4.0 metadata. The applicable
distribution terms have not been clarified; this archive makes no legal determination.
The text is therefore withheld from distribution while the discrepancy is unresolved.

The source describes two text editions, identifies edition 2 as “Text of the EOTC
printed Bible,” and does not identify the print exemplar or date. The local audit
record reports 19 chapter divisions and 463 numbered line elements in that capture.
Those counts do not establish completeness, transcription accuracy, or identity with
the Church catalogue's grouped “Metsihafe Tibeb (books of wisdom)” entry.
