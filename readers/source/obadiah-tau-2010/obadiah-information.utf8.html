Information and Notes: Obadiah

 

Bibliographic reference: Bachmann, Johannes (ed.). Dodekapropheton Aethiopum oder die zwölf kleinen Propheten der aethiopischen Bibelübersetzung. Heft I: Der Prophet Obadia, Halle a.S., 1892.

 

This is an internet image version of the Ethiopic Book of Obadiah.  It follows Johannes Bachmann’s critical edition published in Halle in 1892. Johannes Bachmann, a German orientalist, lived between 1863–1894 (not to be confused with the better-known theologian of the same name, 1832–1888).

Bachmann’s Textkritische Anmerkungen are available at the bottom of the menu, under the plus-sign (+).

The TEXT version of this book was transcribed by Samuel Aldridge, Jeremy Brown, Steve Delamarter, Garry Jost, Shaun Short for the Textual History of the Ethiopic Old Testament (THEOT) Project, co-directed by Steve Delamarter and Curt Niccum (February, 2013).
