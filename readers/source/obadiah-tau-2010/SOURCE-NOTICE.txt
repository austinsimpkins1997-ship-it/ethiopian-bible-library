Obadiah source capture and reuse notice
Captured 2026-10-25.

Source edition: Johannes Bachmann (ed.), Dodekapropheton Aethiopum oder die zwölf kleinen Propheten der aethiopischen Bibelübersetzung, Heft I: Der Prophet Obadia, Halle a.S., 1892. The source information page says the digital TEXT transcription was prepared by Samuel Aldridge, Jeremy Brown, Steve Delamarter, Garry Jost, and Shaun Short for the Textual History of the Ethiopic Old Testament (THEOT) Project in February 2013.
