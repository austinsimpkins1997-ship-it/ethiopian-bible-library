This scope includes the project-authored portions of:

- `archive.json`, `canon-registry.json`, `translation-registry.json`, `book-coverage.json`, `package-inventory.json`, and `language-script-code-audit.json`;
- machine-readable examples and derived reports under `examples/`, including the registered archive graph, review coverage, and portable-layer manifest;
- project-authored research catalogues, review notes, and reports under `research/`;
- the same project-curated metadata when embedded in `index.html`.

The license covers original annotations and database selection or arrangement where those rights exist. Reader JSON files and source captures are not covered by this metadata license; consult `CONTENT-LICENSES.md` and the individual source or reader record.

Suggested attribution for reusable project metadata: **Ethiopian Bible Library contributors**, identify the record IDs and source IDs, cite the package version or commit when available, and describe changes. Retain third-party citations and required notices. CC BY 4.0 does not create rights in material the project itself cannot license.
