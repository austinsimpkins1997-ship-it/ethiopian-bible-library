# Job source review

The [Beta maṣāḥǝft work record](https://betamasaheft.awhamburg.de/works/LIT1688Job/main) identifies the Geʽez title ኢዮብ፡ (ʾIyob, Book of Job), normalized as Maṣḥāfa ʾIyob. It lists Pereira 1905 in the editions bibliography and reports 31 manuscript records containing the work. Its separate [text view](https://betamasaheft.awhamburg.de/works/LIT1688Job/text) is a discovery lead; the text was not copied or structurally audited.

The text page states that the file is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and separately displays a site-level CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 notice. The relationship between these notices and rights in the underlying edition or manuscripts is unresolved. Under the archive's [content policy](../CONTENT-LICENSES.md), no wording or TEI is bundled until terms are clarified.

This source is separate from the existing Pereira Job lead: Beta maṣāḥǝft cites Pereira 1905, while the Biblioteca Nacional de Portugal bibliography dates the Patrologia Orientalis II contribution to 1907. The entries are not assumed to describe an identical release. The shared title supports only an uncertain candidate link to Church-list Job; it does not establish the EOTC edition, scope, completeness, or accuracy.
