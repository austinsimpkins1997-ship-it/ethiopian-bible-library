# Nara scripture-source distinction

**Review date:** 2026-10-26  
**Scope:** Distinguish Eritrean Nara (`nrb`) from a similarly named but unrelated Papua New Guinea language/source; document bounded catalogue findings. This note is metadata research, not a scripture translation or a language-wide census.

## Findings

- The official eBible.org PDF titled *Luva'ana'i Namana Makamakana* identifies its text as the New Testament in Nara (Lala), Central Province, Papua New Guinea, and gives language code `nrz`. It is not the Eritrean Nara language (`nrb`) and is excluded from the Eritrea profile and reader corpus. Its stated CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 terms are recorded only for source identification; no text or illustrations are copied into this archive.
- The eBible.org Eritrea territory catalogue inspected on 2026-10-26 has no Nara row. A territory filter is a discovery index, not a census of languages or translations in Eritrea.
- Joshua Project's Nara (`nrb`) profile says translation has started but reports no specific Bible portions, New Testament, complete Bible, or possible print Bible resource. This is a secondary directory report, not evidence that no translation text exists elsewhere.
- An Eritrean Ministry of Information interview published in 2009 describes Nara-language books and reports that Latin-script writing was introduced in 1993. This dated report is contextual evidence about writing and published language materials; it does not establish today's orthographic standard or the availability of scripture.

## Limits and use

These records resolve name/code collisions and preserve where discovery was checked. They do not establish that no Nara scripture exists, that a translation project remains active, or which orthography a current Nara community prefers. No new Nara reader or canon link is created; the bounded survey result stays at zero directly accessible scripture resources in the named provider indexes.
