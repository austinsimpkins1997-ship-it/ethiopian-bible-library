# Gitsew source review

The [Ethiopian Orthodox Bible Project's 2017 report](https://ethiopianorthodoxbible.wordpress.com/2017/08/12/progress-we-have-acquired-the-book-of-gitsew/) says its team acquired a complete Gitsew text in Geʽez and Amharic, purchased as a book in Ethiopia, and describes it as one of the four Sinodos books. The project's [canon page]({CANON_URL}) lists Gitsew as broader New Testament entry 30. The page also carries the project's copyright notice: Copyright © 2017–2025, All Rights Reserved.

This is a discovery lead, not a reader. The physical book, title page, edition/imprint, photographs, text, boundaries, completeness, accuracy, and book-text reuse terms were not inspected. The website's copyright notice applies to that page; it does not by itself determine rights in the underlying book. No wording or images were copied, and the lead does not increase reader coverage.

Lexham Bible Dictionary lists Gessew among the four Sinodos works and summarizes reported manuscript variation; this supports contextual naming, not identification of the acquired physical book. See the existing source record SRC-211. The candidate link to the Church-list Gitsew entry remains uncertain.
