Genesis 1: first- and third-person references

A manually bounded lexical pilot over the archive’s stored English companion and Geʽez chapter text. These figures describe counted strings under stated rules; they are not a theological conclusion, a count across Genesis, or a universal-language translation.

Read the language boundary first. The English observations below do not describe Geʽez grammar. The Geʽez divine-name total is an exact-string count only. A Geʽez first-person verb count remains unassessed because the archive has not validated a morphology and speaker-attribution method for this chapter.

Interpretive limits

  • The three English first-person forms are counted as grammar only. This record does not decide the theological meaning of “us” or “our.”
  • Attributing exact speech excerpts to God is an editorial judgment and is marked unreviewed in the machine-readable record.
  • The stored English witness is a specific companion text. Different English editions may use different words; this is not a language-neutral count.
  • The Geʽez first-person total is unassessed, not zero. Searching for independent pronouns would miss person marking carried by verbs.
  • Matching verse labels do not establish translation equivalence. No machine-generated wording is presented as scripture.
