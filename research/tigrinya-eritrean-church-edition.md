# Eritrean Tigrinya deuterocanonical Bible catalogue record

**Inspected:** 2026-10-27  
**Catalogue:** [Four Corners of the World Library, publication 168](https://fourcornersoftheworld.ptrs.ru.nl/publication/168/public/)

The catalogue gives the title *Metshaf Kidus Kaliay Quenona Beluy Kidan Zihaze*, supplies the English rendering ‘The Revised Bible with Deuterocanonical Books in Tigrinya,’ and identifies Bible Society of Eritrea, Asmara, 2000, ISBN 978-9966-40-984-3 / CLDC062. It associates the item with the Eritrean Orthodox Tewahdo Church and reports that Orthodox Tewahdo, Catholic, and Evangelical/Lutheran churches participated in the edition; for translating the deuterocanonical books, it says only Orthodox Tewahdo and Catholic participants took part. The record lists 286 pages without maps and 291 with maps.

A bookseller page identifies a product with the same ISBN and CLDC062 code as a 2010 item. This conflicts with the library catalogue's 2000 date; no physical title page has been inspected to resolve the difference. Another seller page lists a similar-titled 2010 CLDC032 item with a different ISBN (978-9966-27-384-0) and claims 1,400 pages. That description is not verified against a physical volume and does not establish that CLDC032 and CLDC062 are the same edition or which books either contains.

These are catalogue/seller statements, not direct inspection of printed volumes. No contents list or scripture text was obtained. The archive has not established the book-by-book contents, translation basis, accuracy, completeness, equivalence to another Tigrinya Bible, or a crosswalk to the Ethiopian Church's 81 listed entries. No reuse license was found; rights remain unknown and neither text is bundled.

These records add bibliographic leads only. They do not increase reader or canon-coverage counts. Archive source records: `SRC-438`, `SRC-439`, and `SRC-440`.
