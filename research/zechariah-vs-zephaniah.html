Zechariah vs. Zephaniah

These are two distinct prophetic books. Their similar English names are easy to confuse; their place in the Church’s list, their source pages, and this archive’s text availability are different.

Why positions 41 and 43? The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church’s own English list places Zephaniah at 41, Haggai at 42, and Zechariah at 43. They are separate entries with Haggai between them—not alternate spellings of one book.
