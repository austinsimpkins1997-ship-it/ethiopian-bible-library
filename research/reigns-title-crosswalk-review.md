# Reigns / Samuel / Kings title crosswalk review

**Scope:** work/title crosswalk only. This review does not identify a historical print witness with a current Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church (EOTC) edition, and it does not establish text accuracy or completeness.

## Evidence compared

The EOTC's English canon list gives one grouped entry, **“I and II Samuel”** at Old Testament position 9, and another, **“I and II Kings”** at position 10. Tel Aviv University's information page describes the digitized Ethiopic Books of Reigns I–IV as “Samuel I–II and Kings I–II,” and cites Dillmann's fascicles: *Regum I et II* (1861) and *Regum III et IV* (1871). The archive compared these names and groupings and records reviewed work/title crosswalks to those two Church-list entries. TAU remains the source of the historical Reigns-to-Samuel/Kings identification; this review does not independently establish recension or edition identity.
