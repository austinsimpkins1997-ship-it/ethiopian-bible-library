Reported English editions and partial translation

Sinodos sections

The Ethiopian Orthodox Bible Project's announcement dated 2025-11-05 says it released an English edition of the Ethiopic Books of the Synod (Sinodos). It reports that the edition uses Horner's The Statutes of the Apostles (1904), which the post associates with these sections, and compares versions:

  • Sirate Tsion (Book of Order): the announcement describes a section in the released English Sinodos edition and reports Horner 1904 as a basis. Exact wording, book boundaries, and the edition's relationship to the Church-list entry remain unverified.
  • Tizaz (Book of Herald): the announcement describes a section in the released English Sinodos edition and reports Horner 1904 as a basis. Exact wording, book boundaries, and the edition's relationship to the Church-list entry remain unverified.
  • Abtilis: the announcement describes a section in the released English Sinodos edition and reports Horner 1904 as a basis. Exact wording, book boundaries, and the edition's relationship to the Church-list entry remain unverified.

The post also names Schodde's 1885 English translation of the Ethiopic Gessew as a basis for the Canons section. That is consistent with the separately documented Schodde witness, but does not prove that this newly reported edition is the same as any other publisher's modernized Gitzew edition. No direct Ge'ez-to-English path is assigned to the Sinodos sections in these records.

Partial Clement rendering

The same announcement reports an English rendering of Clement 6:83-86. It says the manuscript it calls Amharic has damaged or lost wording, that Ethiopic fragments and a French translation were consulted, and that verse 83 was translated solely from French because the surviving Ethiopic is unintelligible. This report does not establish a full Book of Clement translation, source alignment, or its exact relationship to the Church-list title.

Source, publication, and rights limits

The announcement is the primary source for what the Ethiopian Orthodox Bible Project claims about its own edition. It links to sales listings, but the linked listings and books were not independently inspected. The page carries a copyright notice and offers no reuse license for its text or the books.
