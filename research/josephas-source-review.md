# Book of Josephas the Son of Bengorion — source review

The [EOTC English list](https://www.ethiopianorthodox.org/english/canonical/books.html?lang=en) gives this title as entry 46. [Beta maṣāḥǝft CAe 2598](https://betamasaheft.awhamburg.de/works/LIT2598Yosipp/main) catalogs the Ethiopic Josippon / Zenā ʾAyhud work family and records multiple attributions; it does not identify the record as the Church-list entry. The [British Library Or 824 catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-003440580) describes a Geʽez history of the Jews ascribed to Joseph ben Goryon. A separately catalogued [Or 822 witness](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-003451655) dates to the 17th century and covers ff. 2r–136v, with a colophon at f. 136r; the Or 824 catalogue dates to the 18th century and gives ff. 3r–136v. The different records and folio descriptions are retained separately.

The [Ethiopian Orthodox Bible Project Committee's 2017 report](https://ethiopianorthodoxbible.wordpress.com/2017/10/24/progress-we-have-acquired-the-book-of-josippon/) says it acquired a printed Zëna Ayhud/Josippon volume and identifies it as the greater-canon work. This is the project's own assertion, not an EOTC-issued edition record or independent text collation; publication details and text reuse rights remain unknown. It adds an attributed discovery lead but no reader coverage.

A current external English edition is described by [The Ethiopian Canon Project](https://www.ethiopiancanon.com/blog/how-we-rebuilt-the-book-no-one-has-translated) and its [sources/methodology page](https://www.ethiopiancanon.com/sources). The publisher says its Josippon reconstruction approximates the Ethiopian text's scope and ordering, has 116 chapters, and is built primarily through Latin witnesses to Hebrew Sefer Yosippon with a dissertation's crosswalk; it explicitly says the result is not a direct Geʽez translation. These are publisher claims: no print copy, complete chapter structure, underlying source collation, translation accuracy, or reuse license for the full edition has been independently reviewed. The site bears a © 2026 notice, which does not itself date publication. It is recorded as an external, source-asserted edition lead only; no text is copied or bundled.

The tempting 1718/1722 English title [The Wonderful and Most Deplorable History of the Later Times of the Jews](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL42749782W/The_wonderful_and_most_deplorable_history_of_the_later_times_of_the_Jews) is not a complete English Ethiopian Josippon. Open Library's edition note and the [University of Pennsylvania's Judaica Americana bibliography](https://singerman.judaicadhpenn.org/entry/0020/) identify it as Peter Morwen's rendering of Abraham ibn Daud's abridged Josippon account, in its book-3 / Sefer ha-kabalah context. It is recorded as a related text, not as a reader or crosswalk. No digitized text was inspected or copied.

These are discovery leads, not a verified crosswalk. Or 822, Or 823, Or 824, Beta maṣāḥǝft's listed Josippon witnesses, and the separate EAP704/2/3 manuscript must not be merged. The British Library provides a digitized-content link but says access restrictions apply; its image-use statement is not a blanket license. Beta maṣāḥǝft's record displays differing license notices. No text or images are bundled, and no authorial attribution, chapter structure, complete extent, or exact Church-list identity is asserted. The added contemporary reconstruction remains attributed and unverified; neither it nor the historical abridgement is bundled or counted as canon-linked reader coverage.
