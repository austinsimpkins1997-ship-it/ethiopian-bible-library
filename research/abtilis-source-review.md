# Abtilis source review

[Beta maṣāḥǝft's work record](https://betamasaheft.awhamburg.de/works/LIT2677ACABt1/main) catalogs Apostolic canons Abṭelisāt (81), CAe 2677. Its [Maṣḥafa ṭefut manuscript record](https://betamasaheft.awhamburg.de/manuscripts/AGmasTefut1/main) labels ff. 174v–177r as the first recension (81); the same manuscript separately lists Apostolic canons (56) on ff. 172v–174v. Other catalogue records show recension variation, including an 80-canon second recension. These are distinct catalogue units and counts; the archive does not collapse them into one text.

The record is a discovery lead for the Church-list Abtilis title, not an exact canon crosswalk. No manuscript text or image was captured. Beta displays a file-level CC BY-SA 4.0 notice and a site-level CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 notice; their relationship and underlying manuscript rights remain unresolved. The lead is unbundled and does not increase reader coverage.
