What categories mean here

Open license stated
A license is stated in the record; its exact terms and scope still control.
Public domain claimed
A source or record claims public-domain status; the archive has not established a worldwide legal conclusion.
Permission limited
Use is recorded with permission or other specific limits; follow the detailed notice.
All rights reserved
The source records an all-rights-reserved notice; no broader permission is inferred.
Mixed terms or jurisdictions
The record contains differing rights statements, jurisdictions, or components that must remain distinct.
Unknown / not established
The record does not establish reuse terms. Unknown is neither permission nor a finding that reuse is forbidden.

Limits

  • A source-record count is not a count of works, translations, or reader texts.
  • A reader-record count is not a count of unique books or complete editions; several records may describe separate language or edition layers.
  • An open-source software license or metadata license does not relicense scripture, translations, scans, images, or other third-party content.
