Richard Laurence’s 1883 English 1 Enoch

Bibliographic and rights evidence only. This page does not contain a reader or a transcription.

What the source establishes

  • Project Gutenberg identifies Richard Laurence (1760–1838) as translator.
  • The printed title describes the work as translated from an Ethiopic manuscript in the Bodleian Library.
  • The recorded original publication is London, Kegan Paul, Trench & Co., 1883.
  • The Gutenberg item marks its eBook public domain in the United States.

What remains unresolved

The title does not identify the exact manuscript shelfmark or recension in the records inspected. It does not establish a chapter map, completeness, wording accuracy, or identity with a current Ethiopian or Eritrean Orthodox edition. It is a title-level match to the Church-list entry “Enoch,” not proof that this translation represents that edition.

The 1883 preliminary matter says translation and reproduction rights are reserved. Project Gutenberg separately states a U.S.-scoped public-domain claim and tells readers elsewhere to check local law. This archive records both notices and does not resolve their legal interaction or claim worldwide reuse rights. No text is bundled.
