TAU Platt 1830 New Testament menu audit

Local capture date: 2026-10-26. TAU identifies its digitized Ethiopic New Testament as following Platt’s 1830 edition and says book/chapter navigation is available from the menu. This page records observed menu metadata; it is not a reader or a canon crosswalk.

Rights and boundaries. TAU's digitized-text notice permits noncommercial copying/distribution only with required notices. Its Pauline and Catholic Epistles pages separately restrict Ted Erho's transcriptions to personal research and prohibit repository storage without written consent. No transcription or scan image is bundled here.
