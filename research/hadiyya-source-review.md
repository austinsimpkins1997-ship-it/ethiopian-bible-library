# Hadiyya scripture-source and rights review

**Review date:** 2026-10-26  
**Scope:** Record observed Hadiyya (`hdy`) online scripture references, distinguish the editions and interfaces, and state what is and is not established. No scripture text is reproduced here.

## Findings

- The Digital Bible Society archive record HDYHDH identifies a Hadiyya Bible published in 1992 by the Bible Society of Ethiopia, in Ethiopic script, with provider scope `FB` (full Protestant canon). Its record links to Bible.com and EthiopiaScripture.org. This is edition metadata, not a verified EOTC canon mapping or independent completeness check.
- A separate Digital Bible Society record, HDYHDY, identifies a Hadiyya New Testament published in 1993 by the Bible Society of Ethiopia, in Ethiopic script. The archive keeps this as a distinct catalogue record; it does not assume whether its text is identical to the 1992 Keesammi Kitaaba or the later digital text.
- Bible.com identifies version 3498 as *KEESAMMI KITAABA* in Hadiyya and exposes a text page for Deuteronomy 2. This verifies online access to at least a sample from the Old Testament in that version; it does not audit every book or establish physical-edition completeness.
- EthiopiaScripture.org's Hadiyya page displays a New Testament book/chapter navigation and attributes the text to © Bible Society of Ethiopia, 2021. The page does not provide a redistribution license. Its relationship to the 1992 Bible.com/DBS record is not established; do not merge them as one edition or assume the 2021 page licenses text reuse.
- Scripture Earth independently lists Hadiyya (`hdy`) and links to Bible.is and Bible.com, but is a discovery index, not rights or edition-authentication evidence.

## Rights and archive decision

The digital texts remain **unbundled**. The 1992 rights record reports Bible Society of Ethiopia copyright and no redistribution grant was found. The 2021 portal states copyright but no open license. Provider terms that govern a reading platform do not by themselves transfer rights to this archive. The GitHub repository's “public domain” label is not supported by an item-level rights grant and remains excluded as permission evidence.

## Limits

The current discovery survey records three provider resource records/interfaces: the 1992 Keesammi Kitaaba Bible, the separate 1993 Hadiyya New Testament record, and the EthiopiaScripture.org 2021-copyright New Testament interface. The 1992 Bible.com text is one online presentation of the full-Bible record, not an additional edition count. The 1993 and 2021 New Testament relationship to the 1992 Bible and to each other is unresolved. The archive does not claim a verified translation lineage, all-book collation, accuracy, dialect/community review, or a crosswalk to Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo canon entries. No reader records or book-coverage counts are added.
