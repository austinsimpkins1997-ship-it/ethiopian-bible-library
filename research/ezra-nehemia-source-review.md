# Ezra and Nehemia source review

## Work identification

The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church's English list groups **Ezra and Nehemia** as one of its 81 entries (Old Testament entry 15). Tel Aviv University's [book-specific notes](https://www.tau.ac.il/~hacohen/Ezra/Information.html) identify the displayed Geʽez work as F. M. Esteves Pereira's 1919 *Le troisième livre de ‘Ezrâ (Esdras et Néhémie canoniques)*. They distinguish this canonical Ezrâ III from the separately numbered Ethiopic Ezra I and II works. This supports a scoped historical work/title match to the Church-list wording; it does not establish identity with a current EOTC printed edition.

## What the digital evidence establishes

The live [TAU Ethiopic Bible index](https://www.tau.ac.il/~hacohen/Biblia.html) was inspected on 2026-10-26. Its form titled “Ezra and Nehemia” displayed 23 menu options labeled 1–23, targeting page wrappers in the source order recorded in [the menu audit](ezra-nehemia-menu-audit.json). The response was 111,977 bytes with SHA-256 `0686b54eeb4f6d69e247b19eb1d03f69154f5c006a60be456402538a91e31c70`. TAU says each chapter is accessible from this menu.

The digitized [Pereira edition PDF](https://www.patristique.org/sites/patristique.org/IMG/pdf/po_66_xiii_5.pdf) is a 98-page scan of the 1919 edition in a 1988 facsimile reprint. Its printed text is numbered I–XXIII: Ezra ends at chapter X and Nehemiah begins at XI. The 23 ordered TAU targets are consistent with this print structure, but were not individually matched to print pages. Pereira explicitly says manuscript chapter divisions vary. For d'Abbadie MS 35 he reports 21 chapters, while his introductory prose and following chapter-to-Greek-verse table differ about the precise Ezra/Nehemiah boundary; for British Museum Add. 16.188 he says the text has no chapter divisions. These are edition- and witness-specific structures. This review does not prove every page is present or textually accurate, or that the edition matches a current EOTC printed Bible. No local scan, OCR, or scripture wording was created.

## Rights and archive treatment

TAU's [site-wide notice](https://www.tau.ac.il/~hacohen/Information.html) grants permission to use, copy, and distribute digitized Ethiopic texts for noncommercial purposes when its copyright and permission notices accompany each copy. This is conditional permission, not an open license. The displayed pages include a French translation; the rights scope for reproducing the images and French text was not resolved. The archive therefore links to TAU but bundles no page images, OCR, or scripture wording. The catalogue records the external lead and its limits; reader coverage does not increase.
