# Sirate Tsion source review

The [Mahibere Kidusan Church Sunday School Department page](https://eotcmk.org/e/holy-scriptures/) lists Sirate Tsion as New Testament entry 28 and states the list has 46 Old Testament and 35 New Testament entries. It cites Church publications from 1983 and 1970. This supports the title and placement in that displayed list, not the identity of any particular manuscript witness.

Beta maṣāḥǝft's [work record](https://betamasaheft.awhamburg.de/works/LIT2673CanonsAscension/main) identifies the Geʽez unit as Canons of the Apostles after Ascension, CAe 2673. Its [British Library Oriental 794 record](https://betamasaheft.awhamburg.de/manuscripts/BLorient794/main) places a 29-canon witness at folios 71v–77v in a manuscript dated palaeographically to 1400–1450.

Cowley and the Lexham Bible Dictionary report Serʽatä Seyon (Sirate Tsion) as a 30-canon broader-canon title and suggest it may correspond to Canons after the Ascension. Lexham explicitly says the relation is unclear. The witness title is different and its count is 29, so the archive records a candidate only and preserves the discrepancy. No text or image is bundled.

This catalogue record is encoded from manuscript catalogues; the archive did not inspect the codex, images, or wording. The candidate does not verify the Church-list crosswalk, textual boundaries, completeness, or reuse rights.
