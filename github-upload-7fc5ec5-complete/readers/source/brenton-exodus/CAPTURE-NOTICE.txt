Brenton Septuagint Exodus capture notes

Forty browser-visible text extracts were saved from eBible.org chapter pages. The captures contain the page's visible wording, labels, note markers, and extracted translator notes; raw HTML and original response bytes were not retained.

The offline reader exposes this material as an unreviewed research transcription of a named published English translation. Its 40 page headings, ordering, local checksums, extracted labels, nonempty units, and captured notes are checked. Those checks do not establish exact wording against the live page or print edition, textual completeness, translation accuracy, or alignment with the Geʽez witness. It is not labeled verified scripture.
