# Wisdom of Solomon upstream capture

The original Beta maṣāḥǝft `LIT2516Wisdom.xml` is retained here for local audit. Its SHA-256 is
`C2E15DA4893BA597BBF6AFEE3FB5DDBEAC44C02A317A0B4384B4C2624CEA5747`; it is pinned to revision
[`7c13d460bef940950b9405320acc34b9d23a2d92`](https://github.com/BetaMasaheft/Works/blob/7c13d460bef940950b9405320acc34b9d23a2d92/2001-3000/LIT2516Wisdom.xml).

The TEI file says it is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, but specifically states that the text in edition 1 is copyright
1995 Library of Ethiopian Texts and grants only noncommercial copying/distribution with a no-sale condition. Do not
redistribute this full capture as part of the public site. The site contains an ed2-only TEI extraction at
`site/readers/source/wisdom-of-solomon-LIT2516Wisdom-ed2-only.xml` (SHA-256
`1FB77C264B07F5113B84A207EEA4E1A2AEDC9BF55BFEB3EE9631F44369019B93`) and excludes all ed1 text. Ed2's relation to
the Church's grouped “Metsihafe Tibeb (books of wisdom)” catalogue entry remains unresolved.
