# Text, translation, and metadata rights

The repository's `LICENSE` applies to its software code. Project-curated metadata in the files listed by `DATA-LICENSE.md` is separately licensed under CC BY 4.0, only to the extent contributors hold rights they can license. Neither license grants blanket permission to reuse scripture transcriptions, translations, manuscript images, or other third-party content. Each text keeps the rights and attribution stated by its own source record.

## Source-record reuse status

Every source record carries a structured reuse category and status. `unknown` means this archive has not established reuse permission for that cited source record; it is not a public-domain or open-license claim. A citation is not a license. Check the linked source's current item-level terms before reusing its material. Rights for bundled reader text and media are recorded separately on the reader or content record; a source's `unknown` category does not replace those item-specific notices. Every bundled reader also records its controlled `rights_classification`, `rights_status`, exact `copyright_notice`, and `commercial_use` scope. Where a reader previously lacked `rights_status`, the build now derives a concise scoped summary from its existing notice fields without changing the classification or source notice; the reader view shows the summary and the item notice.

**Beta maṣāḥǝft license context:** The Works TEI snapshots state file-level CC BY-SA 4.0. Current Beta maṣāḥǝft Guidelines describe project data and remixes as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; the University of Hamburg's 2022 version 5.2 repository record carries dataset-level CC BY 4.0 metadata. The archive records all three notices, marks affected readers as `mixed`, and does not decide which notice controls or claim rights in unidentified printed exemplars. These terms remain unresolved; do not infer commercial or unrestricted permission. See `research/betamasaheft-license-audit.md`, the unsent `research/betamasaheft-rights-clarification-request.md`, and source records SRC-296–SRC-297.

**Wisdom of Solomon:** The related `LIT2516Wisdom` TEI source remains a research lead only. Its text and generated reader have been removed from this public package while the distribution terms are unresolved. The source metadata and local structural counts are retained in the archive record; they do not imply that the catalogue's grouped “Metsihafe Tibeb (books of wisdom)” entry is Wisdom of Solomon. See `readers/source/WISDOM-SOURCE-NOTICE.txt` and SRC-177.

## Gitsew / Gessew historical source facsimiles

The repository bundles Winand Fell's 1871 *Canones Apostolorum Aethiopice* scan (Ethiopic/Latin) and George H. Schodde's 1885 English translation, pp. 61–72. Theological Commons marks the Fell item's image rights “No Known Copyright”; JSTOR states that, to its knowledge, the 1885 issue is in the public domain. The archive records both as `public_domain_claimed`, not as an open-license grant. Keep their bibliographic attribution with any copy. These are source facsimiles only: no proofread structured transcript, Ge'ez-English alignment, or equivalence claim for every current Church edition is included. See `research/gitsew-fell-schodde-translation.html` and records SRC-214 and SRC-433.

## Required for every new text or translation

Record the exact source/edition, original and target language tags, translator/transcriber, translation basis (including relay languages), chapter/verse boundaries, completeness checks, source citations, license text/URL, required attribution, and any restrictions. If rights are unclear or conflicting, do not bundle the text until resolved. “Free to read” and “openly licensed for reuse” are different permissions.

Language identifiers use BCP 47 tags where available. Script and text direction are metadata; they must not be inferred from a modern language label alone. Community-specific names, transliteration, and orthography should be retained alongside normalized identifiers.
