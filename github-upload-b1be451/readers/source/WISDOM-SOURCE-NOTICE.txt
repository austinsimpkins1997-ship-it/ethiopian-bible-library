The upstream TEI contains two text editions. Its file-level notice states CC BY-SA 4.0, and it separately identifies
edition 1 as copyright 1995 Library of Ethiopian Texts with noncommercial copying terms and a no-sale restriction.
The published site therefore includes only edition 2 and omits the edition 1 text. The full upstream capture is kept
outside the deployed site in the parent archive's source-captures/betamasaheft-wisdom-2026 directory for local audit;
do not copy it into the public site package. Edition 2 is extracted as a research text under the file's CC BY-SA 4.0
notice, with the attribution, change-notice, and share-alike conditions recorded in the reader metadata.

The source calls edition 2 “Text of the EOTC printed Bible,” but does not identify the physical print exemplar or date.
The bundled extract has 19 chapter divisions and 463 numbered TEI line elements. That structural count does not prove
completeness against print, transcription accuracy, or identity with the Church catalogue's grouped wisdom entry.
