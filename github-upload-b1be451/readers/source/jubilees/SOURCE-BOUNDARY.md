# Jubilees TEI edition boundary

The pinned Beta maṣāḥǝft record `LIT1697Jubilees` contains two distinct Geʽez editions. The publication notice explicitly says the Ran HaCohen transcription is available under CC BY-SA 4.0. The same record labels a second edition `EOTCed` as a “standard printed EOTC Bible edition,” but the specific transcription notice does not separately identify its rights. This archive does not assume that a file-level notice clears every embedded source edition for redistribution.

The published build input, `LIT1697Jubilees-Ran.xml`, was extracted from the pinned source and retains its header, bibliography, and only `xml:id="Ran"`. The original source snapshot is preserved in the parent project at `source-captures/betamasaheft-jubilees-2025/LIT1697Jubilees-original.xml`, outside the Pages site.
