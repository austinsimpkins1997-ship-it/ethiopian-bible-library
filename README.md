TAU's edition notes identify Ethiopic Ezra II with Septuagint Esdras alpha / Vulgate third Esdras, and Ezra I (Apocalypse of Esdras) with Vulgate fourth Esdras. Together these support a provisional title-family crosswalk for the two Church-list names; they do not identify an exact current edition.
